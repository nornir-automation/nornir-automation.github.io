Project Announcement

OpsMill is the New Steward of Nornir

OpsMill is now the steward of the Nornir project. Nornir will remain open source and community-driven: your plugins, scripts, and workflows keep working exactly as they do today, and the framework stays free.

With OpsMill's long-term commitment and resources, the focus now turns to modernizing Nornir and shaping its roadmap together with the community. Nornir's creator, David Barroso, continues to support the project as a Technical Advisor.
